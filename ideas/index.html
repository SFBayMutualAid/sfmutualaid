Pods

Mutual aid ideas.

Pods are small, self-directed groups that come together to care for one another and our communities. There are many ways this care can take shape—here are a few examples:

  • Resource Sharing
  • Seed Libraries
  • Eviction Resistance
  • Immigrant Support/Protection
  • Mental Health Support
  • Skillshares
  • Harm Reduction
  • Food Sharing
  • Childcare Collectives
  • Emergency Preparation
  • Book and Study Groups
  • Repair Cafes
  • Peer Listening
  • Community Bail
  • Prisoner Support
  • Community Clean-ups
  • Free Workshops/Trainings
  • Intergenerational Care
  • Lending Libraries
  • Tech Support

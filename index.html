We are a collective of Bay Area residents and organizers meeting the needs of our local community. We currently run two projects: Volunteer-Matching and Crews.

Volunteer-Matching

We match neighbors who need help with neighbors who can give it, and send funds directly to people in need. That means help paying for rent, utilities, medications, and other essentials; deliveries of groceries, medicine, and supplies for elderly, immunocompromised, and high-risk folks; connections to relevant resources in their communities; and tech support with delivery apps, video calls, and more. Ask for help. Offer help. Both are welcome.

Crews

Crews is a new project of SF Bay Mutual Aid. Its purpose is to build deep community through small groups.

A crew is made up of three to eight people. At this size, scheduling is a text message instead of a project, and people come to know each other's histories and patterns. Each crew picks its own focus, whether that's political, social, artistic, or educational. Connection and community aren't just a means to an end here. They're the point.

Three large signal groups, each covering one part of the Bay, exist to help neighbors find and create crews near them.

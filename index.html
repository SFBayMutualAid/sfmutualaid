Volunteer-Matching

We match neighbors who need help with neighbors who can give it, and send funds directly to people in need. That means help paying for rent, utilities, medications, and other essentials; deliveries of groceries, medicine, and supplies for elderly, immunocompromised, and high-risk folks; connections to relevant resources in their communities; and tech support with delivery apps, video calls, and more. Ask for help. Offer help. Both are welcome.

Crews

A crew is a small group of three to eight neighbors: small enough to fit around one kitchen table. Small groups are easier to schedule, you really get to know each other, and everyone is missed when they're not there.

Each crew picks its own focus, whether that's political, social, artistic, or educational. Connection and community aren't just a means to an end here. They're the point.

Three congregations, each covering one part of the Bay, exist to help neighbors find and create crews near them. Join the Signal group for your area:
